# Enhanced Analysis in Deleometer

This document explains the enhanced analysis features in Deleometer, which provide deeper insights using multiple psychological and philosophical frameworks, including Freudian psychoanalysis, Lacanian psychoanalysis, Deleuzian schizoanalysis, Irigarayian psychoanalysis, Jungian analytical psychology, Attachment theory, Positive psychology, Narrative psychology, Phenomenology, Existentialism, Feminist philosophy, Critical theory, Posthumanism, and Buddhist philosophy, along with personalized next steps recommendations focused on happiness.

## Overview

The enhanced analysis system consists of five main components:

1. **User Profile System** - Tracks user patterns over time and enables personalized analysis
2. **Enhanced Analysis Frameworks** - Provides deeper analysis using multiple psychological and philosophical frameworks
3. **Adaptive Learning System** - Improves analysis over time based on user feedback and patterns
4. **Next Steps Recommendations** - Provides personalized, actionable recommendations focused on happiness
5. **Enhanced Visualization** - Presents analysis results in an intuitive, visually appealing format

## User Profile System

The User Profile System tracks your patterns over time to enable personalized analysis. It maintains a profile that includes:

- Emotional baseline (average emotional states)
- Personality baseline
- Common themes and topics
- Recurring patterns
- Edge cases
- Analysis preferences

This profile is used to tailor the analysis to your specific patterns and preferences.

## Enhanced Analysis Frameworks

The Enhanced Analysis Frameworks provide deeper insights using multiple theoretical frameworks:

### Freudian Psychoanalysis

Freudian analysis examines:

- **Id/Ego/Superego dynamics** - The interplay between your unconscious desires, conscious self, and internalized moral standards
- **Defense mechanisms** - Psychological strategies to protect yourself from anxiety
- **Unconscious desires** - Hidden motivations and wishes
- **Complexes** - Organized patterns of feelings, thoughts, and memories
- **Transference** - Projection of feelings onto others
- **Resistance** - Opposition to bringing unconscious material into consciousness

### Lacanian Psychoanalysis

Lacanian analysis explores:

- **Symbolic Order** - The realm of language, law, and culture
- **Imaginary Order** - The realm of images and identifications
- **Real Order** - What escapes symbolization
- **Desire structures** - How desire is structured by lack
- **Signifiers** - Key words or phrases that point to unconscious meaning
- **Jouissance** - Excessive enjoyment or pleasure-pain
- **Lack** - The fundamental absence that drives desire
- **Big Other** - The symbolic order as a social entity
- **Mirror Stage** - The formation of the ego through identification with an image

### Deleuzian Schizoanalysis

Deleuzian analysis investigates:

- **Rhizomatic patterns** - Non-hierarchical, interconnected thought patterns
- **Deterritorialization** - Breaking from established patterns
- **Reterritorialization** - Establishing new patterns
- **Lines of flight** - Paths of creative escape
- **Desire-machines** - Productive connections of desire
- **Body without Organs** - A plane of immanence or consistency
- **Multiplicity** - The coexistence of multiple perspectives
- **Nomadism** - Movement between different conceptual spaces
- **Smooth/Striated spaces** - Open vs. structured spaces of thought

### Irigarayian Psychoanalysis

Irigarayian analysis explores:

- **Sexual Difference** - Analysis of phallocentrism, feminine modes of expression, and mimetic strategies
- **Feminine Subjectivity** - Exploration of fluid identity, embodied knowledge, and relationality
- **Language and Discourse** - Analysis of speaking as woman (parler-femme), disruptive syntax, poetic language, and silences/gaps
- **Ethics of Difference** - Examination of intersubjectivity, wonderment, and mutual respect for difference
- **Key Concepts** - Identification of Irigarayian concepts relevant to your journal entry
- **Thought Patterns** - Analysis of patterns related to sexual difference and feminine subjectivity

### Jungian Analytical Psychology

Jungian analysis explores:

- **Archetypes** - Analysis of the persona, shadow, anima/animus, and self
- **Collective Unconscious** - Exploration of mythological themes and universal symbols
- **Psychological Types** - Analysis of attitude types and cognitive functions
- **Individuation** - Examination of the journey toward self-realization
- **Synchronicity** - Analysis of meaningful coincidences and acausal connections
- **Dreams and Symbols** - Interpretation of symbolic content and dream motifs
- **Key Symbols** - Identification of significant symbols in your journal entry

### Attachment Theory

Attachment analysis examines:

- **Attachment Style** - Analysis of secure, anxious, avoidant, or disorganized patterns
- **Internal Working Models** - Exploration of models of self and others
- **Attachment Behaviors** - Analysis of proximity-seeking, safe haven, and secure base behaviors
- **Relationship Patterns** - Examination of intimacy, dependency, and trust issues
- **Emotional Regulation** - Analysis of coping strategies and emotional expression
- **Growth and Healing** - Exploration of earned security and therapeutic needs
- **Key Attachment Indicators** - Identification of significant attachment indicators

### Positive Psychology

Positive psychology analysis focuses on:

- **Character Strengths** - Identification of signature strengths and virtues
- **PERMA Model** - Analysis of positive emotions, engagement, relationships, meaning, and accomplishment
- **Flow Experiences** - Examination of optimal experiences and barriers to flow
- **Resilience and Growth** - Analysis of resilience factors and growth opportunities
- **Optimism and Hope** - Exploration of explanatory style and future orientation
- **Mindfulness and Gratitude** - Analysis of present-moment awareness and gratitude practices
- **Well-Being Recommendations** - Personalized suggestions for enhancing well-being

### Narrative Psychology

Narrative analysis explores:

- **Narrative Structure** - Analysis of plot type, narrative arc, and coherence
- **Narrative Themes** - Identification of dominant themes and recurrent motifs
- **Narrative Identity** - Exploration of self-concept and identity development
- **Narrative Positioning** - Analysis of agency, communion, and character roles
- **Meaning-Making** - Examination of causal connections and insight generation
- **Cultural Narratives** - Analysis of master narratives and counter-narratives
- **Narrative Recommendations** - Suggestions for narrative development

### Phenomenological Analysis

Phenomenological analysis examines:

- **Lived Experience** - Rich description of the lifeworld and experiential qualities
- **Intentionality** - Analysis of consciousness directedness and content
- **Embodiment** - Exploration of bodily awareness and spatial/temporal experience
- **Intersubjectivity** - Analysis of shared meaning and empathic understanding
- **Bracketing** - Examination of the natural attitude and phenomenological reduction
- **Existential Themes** - Analysis of freedom, authenticity, finitude, and meaning
- **Essential Structures** - Identification of essential structures of experience

### Existentialist Analysis

Existentialist analysis focuses on:

- **Freedom and Responsibility** - Analysis of choice awareness and responsibility
- **Authenticity** - Exploration of authentic self-expression and self-deception
- **Existential Anxiety** - Examination of death, freedom, isolation, and meaninglessness anxiety
- **Absurdity and Meaning** - Analysis of absurdity recognition and meaning creation
- **Being-in-the-World** - Exploration of worldly relations and temporality
- **Interpersonal Existence** - Analysis of being-for-others and authentic relating
- **Existential Recommendations** - Suggestions for existential development

### Feminist Philosophy

Feminist analysis examines:

- **Gender and Power** - Analysis of gender dynamics and power structures
- **Intersectionality** - Exploration of intersecting identities and experiences
- **Situated Knowledge** - Analysis of standpoint and embodied knowing
- **Agency and Autonomy** - Examination of agency expression and constraints
- **Care Ethics** - Analysis of relationality and interdependence
- **Feminist Consciousness** - Exploration of awareness and critical analysis
- **Feminist Recommendations** - Suggestions for feminist development

### Critical Theory

Critical analysis explores:

- **Power Relations** - Analysis of power dynamics and domination patterns
- **Ideology Critique** - Examination of ideological positioning and false consciousness
- **Knowledge/Power** - Analysis of discursive formations and truth regimes
- **Social Structures** - Exploration of structural constraints and systemic patterns
- **Emancipatory Interest** - Analysis of emancipatory potential and communicative action
- **Critical Reflexivity** - Examination of self-reflexivity and positionality
- **Critical Recommendations** - Suggestions for critical development

### Posthumanist Analysis

Posthumanist analysis examines:

- **Human/Nonhuman Relations** - Analysis of more-than-human world relations
- **Technology Relations** - Exploration of technological embodiment and entanglement
- **Distributed Agency** - Analysis of agential assemblages and material agency
- **Ontological Boundaries** - Examination of boundary dissolution and hybrid identities
- **Ethical Considerations** - Analysis of posthuman ethics and multispecies justice
- **Affective Dimensions** - Exploration of more-than-human and technological affect
- **Posthumanist Recommendations** - Suggestions for posthumanist development

### Buddhist Philosophy

Buddhist analysis focuses on:

- **Four Noble Truths** - Analysis of suffering, its origins, cessation, and the path
- **Three Marks of Existence** - Exploration of impermanence, suffering, and non-self
- **Dependent Origination** - Analysis of causal chains and interdependence
- **Mindfulness and Awareness** - Examination of present-moment awareness and mind states
- **Compassion and Loving-kindness** - Analysis of self-compassion and compassion for others
- **Middle Way** - Exploration of balance, moderation, and skillful means
- **Buddhist Recommendations** - Suggestions for Buddhist practice and development

## Next Steps Recommendations

The Next Steps Recommendations feature provides personalized, actionable guidance based on your journal entries:

1. **Identified Goals** - Recognition of both explicit and implicit goals in your writing
2. **Recommended Actions** - Concrete steps to move forward, categorized by:
   - Difficulty (easy, moderate, challenging)
   - Timeframe (immediate, short-term, long-term)
   - Category (emotional, cognitive, behavioral, relational, spiritual, creative)
3. **Potential Obstacles** - Identification of internal and external barriers to progress
4. **Resources and Support** - Suggestions for leveraging existing strengths and finding additional resources
5. **Long-Term Vision** - A positive vision for your future based on your values and aspirations
6. **Happiness Insights** - Personalized insights about what contributes to your happiness, including:
   - Current factors contributing to happiness
   - Potential factors to increase happiness
   - Your personal definition of happiness

## Adaptive Learning System

The Adaptive Learning System improves analysis over time based on:

1. **Feedback-Based Learning** - Adjusts analysis based on your feedback
2. **Pattern Recognition** - Identifies and learns from recurring patterns
3. **Edge Case Adaptation** - Adapts to unusual or edge cases
4. **Theoretical Framework Optimization** - Optimizes the use of theoretical frameworks based on effectiveness

This system ensures that the analysis becomes more accurate and personalized over time.

## Settings

You can customize the enhanced analysis through the following settings:

- **Enable Enhanced Analysis** - Toggle deep analysis using all theoretical frameworks
- **Enable User Profiling** - Toggle user profiling to personalize analysis based on your patterns
- **Enable Adaptive Learning** - Toggle adaptive learning to improve analysis over time
- **Enable Next Steps Recommendations** - Toggle personalized recommendations for next steps
- **Detail Level** - Set the level of detail for analysis (Basic, Detailed, Comprehensive)
- **Theoretical Frameworks** - Select which theoretical frameworks to use for analysis:
  - Freudian Psychoanalysis
  - Lacanian Psychoanalysis
  - Deleuzian Schizoanalysis
  - Irigarayian Psychoanalysis
  - Jungian Analytical Psychology
  - Attachment Theory
  - Positive Psychology
  - Narrative Psychology
  - Phenomenological Analysis
  - Existentialist Analysis
  - Feminist Philosophy
  - Critical Theory
  - Posthumanist Analysis
  - Buddhist Philosophy
- **Next Steps Categories** - Select which categories of next steps to include in recommendations

## Benefits

The enhanced analysis provides several benefits:

1. **Deeper Insights** - Go beyond surface-level emotional analysis
2. **Personalized Analysis** - Receive insights tailored to your specific patterns
3. **Multiple Perspectives** - Gain insights from fourteen complementary theoretical frameworks
4. **Adaptive Learning** - Experience increasingly accurate and relevant analysis over time
5. **Edge Case Detection** - Identify unusual patterns that might be significant
6. **Actionable Recommendations** - Get personalized next steps focused on increasing happiness
7. **Holistic Approach** - Address emotional, cognitive, behavioral, relational, spiritual, and creative dimensions

## Privacy

Your user profile is stored locally in your Obsidian vault and is never shared with external services. The analysis is performed using your API key and the results are stored only on your device.
