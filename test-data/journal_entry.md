# Journal Entry - March 15, 2025

Today I woke up feeling a strange mix of emotions. There was a lingering anxiety about the project deadline next week, but also an unexpected sense of calm. I spent the morning working on the presentation, and found myself getting into a flow state that I haven't experienced in weeks.

During lunch, I had a conversation with Sarah about her recent trip to Japan. As she described the temples and gardens, I felt a strong desire to travel again. It's been over two years since my last international trip, and I'm starting to feel that familiar restlessness.

In the afternoon, I struggled to maintain focus. My mind kept wandering to childhood memories - specifically summers at my grandparents' lake house. I'm not sure why these memories are surfacing now. Perhaps it's because I've been feeling disconnected from nature lately, spending too much time indoors and on screens.

I've been reading "The Unbearable Lightness of Being" before bed. There's a passage about how we only live once, without the ability to compare different choices, that's been haunting me. I wonder how different my life would be if I had taken that job in Seattle five years ago. Would I be happier? More fulfilled? Or would I just have a different set of problems?

Tonight I'm feeling the weight of decisions - both those I've made and those I've avoided making. There's a sense that time is passing too quickly, and I'm not being intentional enough about how I spend it. I need to reflect more on what truly matters to me and align my daily actions with those values.

Tomorrow I plan to wake up early and go for a hike before work. I need to reconnect with nature and clear my head.
